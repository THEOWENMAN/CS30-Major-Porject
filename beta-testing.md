# Beta-Testing

## Non-Programmer's advice:
Pros:
- The multiplayer function makes the game more fun and social. 
- The design or texture of the grid map looks nice.
- The controls are very simple and shooting is easy.
- The obstacles collision detection is well down.

Cons:
- Game is laggy sometimes when there are 3+ people.
- Game feels repetitive and boring after a while.
- Music does not relate to the atmosphere of the game and is too loud.
- Game looks too bland and simple.
- map is too redundant.
- What is the purpose of the smiley face?

## Programmer's advice
Pros:
- Simple easy to understand controls
- Good music selection and sound effects
- Good bullet speed (Fast enough to hit the other person, but also slow enough to be able to dodge)
- I like the bullet fade

Cons:
- The water cell was a little confusing to understand, I did not understand that that texture was meant to be water.
- End goal unclear (What do I get from eliminating the opponent?).
- Why is it ‘C’ to start instead of ‘any key’ or a more universal key?
- Character feels a little slow compared to the size of the map.
- Maybe that could be because of the lack of acceleration. You could consider adding a bit of acceleration and deceleration, but not enough where the controls feel sluggish.
- The menu music is a lot quieter than the gameplay music.
- Health numbers are a little tricky to read, maybe a health bar would be better?
- Moving diagonal is faster than when only moving up, left, right, or down (This could be a gameplay feature however).

## Known Bugs:
- When moving and if a bullet hits, the frames update too slow therfore taking more than 10 HP for one bullet
